My Learning Journey

Land Rover Q and A

Date: 29-10-25

Description: My university hosted Land Rover to come and speak to us and we were sitting at a table and we spoke to Isabelle Topali, who is currently working in project management at range rover, I was given the opportunity to ask questions to her and this resulted in her giving me her linkedIn account, so I can keep up to date with her lifestyle. I asked questions about how demanding the job is and whether it was worth it, as she is on placement there. I also got the chance to speak to Stephen Child who is a lead HV at Land rover. It was very helpful speaking to him because he was able to reassure us that as much as land rover is a well established company, it is still in need of innovative and creative discoveries, and that is where us as the future generation come in, so jobs will never really be low because they also want to open up a proper branch specifically for computer science.

Key Learnings: It meant a lot to me because it gave me hope to want to find a job there. Land Rover is a car company that I have always looked up to but always felt like it is a place out of reach, but when speaking to them, I realized that someone like me can find themselves working there. It helped me develop a sense of commitment and also a good grasp of how my life should be while working in this department.

Reflection: I will follow up on them, but I genuinely want to apply for my placement year to happen with them, as I really like their company and how things work there, and I would be very excited to work there.

Working at Primark

Date: 15-10-25 to 13-12-25

Description: During the activity, I would help with the maintenance of the store by either working at the tills or being placed on the floor. When I was on tills, I was checking people’s items out, collecting payment and putting it into a register. When I worked on the floor, I would pick up clothes that had been dropped and assist customers whenever they needed help.

Key Learnings: It meant a lot to me because not only did it give me an income, but it also helped me understand the tax system and commitment to a job. It helped me develop conversational skills, which will help me in the long run because speaking to people isn’t the easiest task, so being exposed to that really helped me.

Reflection: In many professional settings, I am going to need good conversational skills, and I need to be able to work with people in a team. Primark was able to help with both at the same time.

Reflection on Future Development

Date: 28-12-25

Overall Reflection: My overall learning experience was a bit of rollercoaster. In life, it is so hard to be certain about something, and often that can lead you down so bad paths of not believing in yourself and just the overall fear of failure. My activities help me develop for the future and help me be a lot more confident that I am making the right choice. In the Land Rover meeting, it was an eye-opener that if I want to make progress, I have to be in control of my own life and not let others dictate what choices I need to make.

Future Development: My goals are to further develop my computer science skills, and look into getting a good placement where I can grow with an established company. I plan on learning all these skills because I ultimately have the goal of wanting to help my country, Zimbabwe, by further developing the country's technology. It is a big dream, but nothing is impossible, as long as I put my head and mind into this, I will be able to complete this.
